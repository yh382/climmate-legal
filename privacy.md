# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 29, 2026**
**Status: Draft for pre-launch website use.**

YANU respects user privacy. This policy explains the types of information we expect to process when providing climbing logs, training, maps, community features, and AI coaching.

## Information We May Collect

We may collect account information, profile details, climbing and training logs, uploaded media, location information with permission, community content, membership status, support messages, and technical logs needed for safety, security, and operations.

Health-adjacent training details such as body metrics, injuries, goals, and training preferences are optional unless a specific feature requires them and clearly asks for permission.

## How We Use Information

We use information to create and secure accounts, save climbing records, generate progress analysis, provide maps and discovery features, operate community tools, manage membership access, offer AI-assisted coaching, respond to support requests, and maintain service safety.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate the product, such as cloud hosting, storage, authentication, maps, payment platforms, analytics, safety tooling, and AI model providers.

## China Edition

The mainland China edition has a separate Chinese privacy policy, personal information inventory, third-party service list, and domestic compliance structure. See the [China edition]({{ '/cn/' | relative_url }}) for those materials.

## Your Choices

You may request access, correction, deletion, export, or account closure through the app when available or by contacting support.
