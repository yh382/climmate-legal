Product

A climbing app built for the things climbers repeat every week.

Fast logging first, meaningful feedback second, and community around actual climbing rather than endless scrolling.

01

Climbing log

Capture grade and result in two taps, then add media, notes, style tags, attempts, and location only when they matter.

02

Progress analysis

Turn sessions into trends across volume, intensity, grade range, styles, and consistency.

03

Training library

Follow structured video lessons, workouts, and plans designed around real climbing goals.

04

Maps

Find gyms, routes, areas, saved places, and future location-aware climbing resources.

05

Community

Post sends, comment on sessions, follow friends, and keep the social layer close to climbing.

06

AI coach

Use permissioned training data to get practical suggestions, reflection prompts, and planning support.

Training

Membership is anchored in training value.

YANU's planned paid features focus on training videos, plans, advanced analysis, maps, and AI coaching. Posting and comments are part of the community experience, not the standalone paid product.

Video lessons Movement, strength, recovery, and progression content.
Advanced analysis Long-term trends, training load, grade range, and personalized suggestions.
Maps and places Discovery, filtering, saved places, and future offline-ready resources.

Global And Local

One brand, two hosting realities.

The global site can stay lightweight and product-led. The China edition has its own structure for ICP filing, app filing, licensing notices, personal information disclosures, SDK lists, and domestic deployment.

China Edition

Chinese compliance materials live in a dedicated edition.

The Chinese site is prepared as the future domestic website foundation. It can be moved to a China-hosted domain when the ICP route is ready.

Separate domain path

Global pages stay in English at the root. China-facing copy and legal disclosures live under /cn/.

Domestic hosting ready

The China edition can later be served from a company-owned, ICP-filed domain with domestic cloud hosting.

Legal materials

Privacy, terms, personal information inventory, SDK list, and filing notes are maintained in Chinese.

Business model clarity

Paid value is positioned around training content, maps, advanced analytics, and AI coaching.
